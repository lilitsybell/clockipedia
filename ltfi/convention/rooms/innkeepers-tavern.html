About This Space

Innkeeper's Tavern combines the castle's Great Room and built-in party bar into one large social space designed for hanging out, listening to music, and spending time together.

Pull Up a Chair

Settle in by the ornate brick fireplace, grab something from the bar, put on some music, and stay awhile. Whether the town is celebrating, recovering, or loudly debating what just happened, this is a natural place for everyone to gather.
