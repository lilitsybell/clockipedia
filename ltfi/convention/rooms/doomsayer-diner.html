About This Space

Doomsayer Diner is the castle's main dining space, with comfortable seating for up to 22 people and plenty of room for the town to gather around the table.

Gather Around

The dining room is built for shared meals, long conversations, and the kind of stories that get more dramatic as the night goes on. Its maple slab epoxy river table can also be used as a buffet or additional dining space.
